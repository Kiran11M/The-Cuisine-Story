About us

A little bit of Bengal in Bengaluru

The Cuisine Story started with a simple idea: bring the comforting, mustard-and-fish flavours of a Bengali home kitchen to Bengaluru — without compromise.

Our cooks grind spices in-house, source fresh river fish like bhetki and pabda, and slow-cook every curry the way it's done back home. Alongside our Bengali specialities, you'll find hearty North Indian favourites and Indo-Chinese dishes for everyone at the table.

Whether you're missing home or discovering Bengali food for the first time, pull up a chair. There's always a story on the plate.
